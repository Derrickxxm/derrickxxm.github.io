Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

CognitoBrand is a personal, single-user publishing tool operated by Derrick Xu. It is used only to publish Derrick Xu's own content to his own social media accounts.

What data the tool accesses

  • OAuth access tokens for Derrick Xu's own accounts on X, LinkedIn, YouTube (Google) and Instagram (Meta), used solely to publish his own posts and videos and to read basic performance metrics of those posts.
  • Basic profile identifiers of those same accounts (for example, the account ID needed to publish).

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or process personal data of any other user.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train AI/ML models.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, in files readable only by the operator's user account, and are never committed to source control.

Google API Services

CognitoBrand's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the connected account's security settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). On revocation, stored tokens are deleted from the operator's computer. Deletion requests: derrick.xu84@gmail.com.
