04 / PRINCIPLES

Workflows Compound. Prompts Do Not.

Systems-minded decomposition, evidence before trust, and fail-closed gates.

SYSTEM-MINDED DECOMPOSITION

Every engineering challenge is treated as an explicit architectural pipeline: input state, deterministic transformation, boundary validation, and feedback reconciliation. Prompts are transient; version-controlled workflows compound.
